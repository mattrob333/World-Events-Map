The five that matter most

  1. Anyone can run up paid research

    Research started on every trip page view, including friends opening an invite. The $2/day cap lived in one server's memory and failed under load (60 runs billed $3.90), and scripts could get around the rate limit and the same-origin check.

  2. Share links can be forged

    A friend could vote as the organizer and erase his votes, a crafted link could break the page, and a trip link could carry a fake "Sign in to dope.travel" button.

  3. Share links reveal your music taste

    Blurbs like "Because you listen to hip hop" travelled in the invite link, while the page promised taste stays on your device.

  4. The mood board gets you wrong

    It invented two extra kids, dropped your name and hometown even for its own example sentence, never captured artists, and couldn't be corrected.

  5. "Right now" doesn't know where you are in the trip

    Lunch at 9pm on the last day with no airport, "you're here" while still at JFK, and "Underway" forever after the trip.

What held up

Honest where it counts

  • Distances are labelled indicative; saved places agree on every screen.
  • Every research tab shows its source and fetch time, and photos match their labels.
  • "Hidden gems" is a stated rule; the Events outage is stated plainly.
  • Stays links are sized to the party.

Solid underneath

  • No secrets in the client code or pages; no server-side request forgery.
  • The normal share-and-merge path attributes votes correctly.
  • The redesign: no sideways scroll on phones, no text under 11 px, readable disclaimers, visible focus rings.
  • The globe scroll fix works: plain scroll moves the page, pinch or ⌘/Ctrl zooms.
