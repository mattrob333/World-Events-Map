# Supporting page photography

These photographs appear on the Access, Trips, and NOW pages. Each page also shows a source and license link directly on the photograph. They are editorial place archives, not photographs of the preview offers, sample trip rooms, live venues, or current conditions. The app displays the downloaded Commons thumbnail at a responsive `background-size: cover` crop; no original photo was retouched.
